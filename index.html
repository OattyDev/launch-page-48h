Same Launch Page deliverable as the $299 SKU, priced at $99 one-time for the first paying client only — to get a live case study and proof. After that close, the public price returns to $299 / $499 rush. Order via the GitHub invoice form and select the $99 SKU.

What do you need from me?

Your product URL, who it's for, and the price or CTA you want on the page. The GitHub issue form covers it — no long questionnaires.

How do I pay?

By Wise invoice (or bank transfer). Open a GitHub invoice-request issue with your product URL — you'll get a fixed-scope agreement and Wise invoice as a reply. Work starts when payment clears.

Is this a full website?

No. One landing page — enough to explain the offer and point buyers to the next step. If you need a multi-page site, blog, or app UI, this isn't the right fit.

Who owns the code?

You do. You get the HTML file and the Vercel project. Host it yourself, move it, or edit it later.
